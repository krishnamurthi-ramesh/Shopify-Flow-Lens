Schema-Aware Country Code Check

RULE L-01

Grounded Technical Finding

In Shopify's GraphQL schema, shippingAddress.countryCode strictly normalizes to 2-letter ISO alpha-2 codes ('US', 'CA'). Comparing this field to 3-letter 'USA' evaluates to False on 100% of US domestic orders.
