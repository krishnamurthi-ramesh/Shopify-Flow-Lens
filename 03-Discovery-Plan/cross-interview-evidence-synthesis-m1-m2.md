## 7. Decision recommendation

Choose one after evidence review:

- **Continue discovery:** too little evidence, incidents not comparable, or important unknowns remain.
- **Refine opportunity:** a narrower problem is emerging; recruit participants with that workflow.
- **Advance to prototype test:** a recurring, consequential problem and a feasible data route are both sufficiently supported.
- **Pivot:** the original diagnostic problem is weak, already solved, or infeasible through supported access.
- **Stop:** no sufficiently valuable unmet need identified in the studied context.

## 8. Limits

Two interviews are useful for refining the problem and discovering missing questions. They do not establish prevalence, segment-level generalisability, statistical confidence, or willingness to pay. Do not present the two-person sample as market validation.
